## 1. Event Overview (Overview)

- **Event name**: Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon
- **Slogan**: *"Come and build the future of superintelligence."*
- **Hosts**: Meta & Cerebral Valley
- **Format**: 100% online (Remote / Virtual, open to participants from anywhere in the world)
- **Entry fee**: Free (for selected participants only, capped at **20,000** seats)
- **Key provided resources**:
  - **Meta Model API credit of $150** usable for Meta's latest AI model family (**Muse Spark series**, **Muse Wonder**, **Muse Image**, **SAM 3.1**, **Muse Voice Transcribe**) (for every selected participant, claimable individually per team member account)
  - Partner inference credits for running Meta open-weight models (**Muse Spark Open 816B**, **Muse Glimmer 30B**, and more)
  - Support for **Muse Code**, a terminal-based multi-agent coding tool
  - Live workshops by Meta engineers and partners, plus Discord office hours
- **Total prizes**: **$1,000,000 USD** (all cash prizes, 2 tracks + bonus prizes)

## 3. Tracks and $1,000,000 Prize Structure (Tracks & Prizes)

The main page summarizes only the $1,000,000 total prize and the 2 tracks, but the exact per-track prize details and eligibility were confirmed on the **[Waiver and Official Rules page](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver)**.

## 4. Project Themes (Project Themes)

Regardless of the chosen track (Indie / Startup), every project must fit **one of the two core themes**.

### Theme 1: Build for Your World
> *"Bring Meta's models into your domain. Solve a problem you know firsthand, free up time and mental space, and make something possible that wasn't before."*
- Solve a problem in a domain you know firsthand with Meta's models.
- A practical, domain-grounded product that frees up time and mental space and enables workflows or creative/productivity experiences that were not possible before.

### Theme 2: Push the Frontier
> *"Use Meta's models to tackle challenges in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials and beyond. Give a machine a new capability, accelerate a scientific experiment, or unlock a better way to understand and control a physical system. Show us what becomes possible when these models meet the real world."*
- Solve hard problems in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, new materials, and beyond with Meta models.
- An ambitious project that gives a machine a new capability, accelerates a scientific experiment, or opens a new way to understand and control a physical system.

## 6. Partner Benefits and API Credit Details (Credits & Partners)

### 6.1 Meta Model API Base Credit ($150 USD)
Key conditions confirmed in the `Credit Terms PDF` and `Waiver`:
- **Amount**: **$150 USD** in promotional Meta Model API credit per selected participant
- **Individual claim per team member**: Even for team entries, **each team member may claim individually with their own Meta Model API account** (e.g., a 4-person team: each claims $150 on their own account; cannot be combined into a single account)
- **Claim method and deadline**: Submit name, application email, and Meta Model API Account Team ID via the Claim Form included in the acceptance ("know before you go") email (deadline: **9:00 PM ET, November 4, 2026**). Applied in batch within 2 business days after the request.
- **Account requirements and validity**: Under the Meta Model API standard terms, registering a payment method is required to activate the API, but it is not a mandatory condition for receiving credits or participating in the hackathon itself (credit use is optional and does not affect judging results). Once applied, credits do not expire until the account is terminated and may also be used for purposes outside the hackathon.

### 6.2 Inference Partners Additional Benefits
Three partners provide additional credits and infrastructure so Meta open-weight models (**Muse Spark Open**, **Muse Glimmer**, **Llama**, **SAM**, and more) can be run:
1. **[Baseten](https://www.baseten.co/)**: **$100 credit** and a **2M TPM (tokens per minute) rate limit** for Meta open-weight models to selected participants
2. **[Modal](https://modal.com/)**: **$100 credit** to selected participants — usable not only for serving Meta open-weight models via [Modal Endpoints](https://modal.com/docs/guide/endpoints) but also for [Serverless GPUs](https://modal.com/docs/guide/gpu), [Sandboxes](https://modal.com/docs/guide/sandboxes), and [LLM Training tools](https://modal.com/docs/guide/training-quickstart)
3. **[Fireworks AI](https://fireworks.ai/)**: **$25 credit** for Meta open-weight models to selected participants, plus a live workshop by **[Sinan Ozdemir](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sinan-ozdemir/)**

## 7. Application Review Method and Full Specification of All 15 Questions (Application Overview)

### 7.1 Application Review Criteria (50:50 Weighting)
Applications are evaluated solely on the answers written in the application form under the following two criteria with **equal weight (50% : 50%)**:
1. **Your project idea (50%)**: How clear and feasible the proposed project idea is (clear and feasible), and how well it fits one of the two project themes (`Build for Your World`, `Push the Frontier`)
2. **Your technical skill (50%)**: Technical capability shown in the description of an AI project recently built firsthand — **what you built** and **the techniques you used**. GitHub, LinkedIn, and deployed product links help verification and are optional.
- Eligible applications exceeding the minimum score cutoff are **auto-accepted up to the first 20,000 (Rolling Acceptance)**.
- Questions 13–16 (index-based later survey items) are surveys for improving the developer program and are not reflected in selection evaluation.

## 8. Eligibility and Intellectual Property (IP) Key Clauses (Waiver Summary)

1. **Age and country of residence**:
   - Must be 18 or older and an adult in the country of residence to participate (Korea residents are eligible to participate and win).
   - Sanctioned countries (Belarus, China, Russia, Ukraine, Syria, Cuba, North Korea, Iran, etc.) and Meta/Cerebral Valley employees, contractors, interns, and immediate family members may not participate.
2. **Intellectual property (IP) ownership and license**:
   - **Participant ownership principle**: All intellectual and industrial property rights in and to the Submission **remain with the participant (or team/organization)** (*"the entrant retains ownership of all intellectual and industrial property rights in and to the Submission"*).
   - **Open-source use allowed**: Open-source software/hardware may be included; comply with the applicable open-source licenses and extend/build features on top (so extending the existing MIT-licensed `lottie-motion-studio` as a base is 100% allowed under the official rules).
   - **Promotion license grant**: As a submission condition, a non-exclusive, royalty-free license is granted to the organizers (Meta, Cerebral Valley, and partners) to use/display/distribute submissions for marketing, promotion, and live demo purposes.
3. **Tax notes**:
   - Non-US residents (Korea, etc.) receiving prizes must submit tax forms such as `W-8BEN`; taxes and FX/remittance fees are the winner's responsibility.
